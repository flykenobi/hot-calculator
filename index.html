Canopy & windshield contamination — DO NOT depart if snow accumulated and not removed from the canopy/windshield area. Confirm with ground crew that canopy area and forward fuselage around pitot/static probes are clear of contamination. Snow on top of fuselage may melt and refreeze in ridges around the probes → unreliable airspeed at T/O run, loss of AP/FD/A-THR/characteristic speeds/rudder travel limiter, reversion to manual Alternate Law.
